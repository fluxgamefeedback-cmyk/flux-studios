01 / PREPARE THE TABLE

YOUR GAME.
YOUR MOVE.

108Cards across numbers, actions and powers
4Elements: Fire, Water, Nature and Lightning
7*Suggested starting cards per player
1Goal: empty your hand after every effect resolves

Shuffle the deck. Deal seven cards to each player, face down. Place the remaining cards as the draw pile and turn over a number card to begin the discard pile. Start clockwise. These setup choices are for our physical prototype.

Physical-edition note: Seven starting cards, a number-card opener and clockwise start are proposed tabletop conventions. The original Android guide does not specify those physical setup details. Agree on them before play.
02 / TURN SEQUENCE

FIVE MOMENTS.
ONE MOVE.

  1. READ: Check the face-up card, current element, direction and pending attack.
  2. PLAY OR DRAW: Match a valid card or draw, even when you hold a playable card. A drawn playable card can be played now or kept.
  3. RESOLVE: Apply all actions and Power effects, including changing an element or drawing cards.
  4. DECLARE: Say FLUX! immediately when your hand reaches one card.
  5. ADVANCE: Move to the correct next player only after every effect and decision is settled.
03 / CARD MATCHING

MATCH THE ENERGY.

A card needs one legal relationship with the centre card:

ELEMENTFire matches Fire, Water matches Water and so on
NUMBERAny number 5 matches another 5 across elements
ACTIONFreeze can match Freeze; Flip can match Flip
POWER / WILDPlay an eligible Power or universal Element card
Example: with Water 5 in the centre, a Water card or any 5 is legal. A compatible Power/Wild may also be playable.

06 / INTERACTIVE LAB

CHAIN. COUNTER.
SURVIVE.

Compatible Strike +2, Chaos +4 and Bomb +6 attacks can chain. The new defender faces the full pending amount. A compatible Shield blocks it; Mirror reflects it to the source. Otherwise draw the accumulated penalty.

07 / THE FINAL CARD

ONE CARD LEFT?
CALL FLUX.

When you reach one card, declare FLUX! before the next turn begins. Missing the declaration window adds two cards. Empty-hand victory is confirmed only after all pending effects, attacks and declarations resolve.

Practice the declaration.

The digital game's synchronized CATCH feature is separate; for the physical version, players should agree before starting who can call a missed FLUX declaration.
08 / TABLE QUESTIONS

THE DETAILS.

Can I draw even if I have a legal play?

Yes. Drawing instead of playing is allowed. If the drawn card is playable, you may play it immediately or keep it.

Does a final attack automatically win?

No. Resolve every pending attack and choice before final victory is awarded.

Can attacks be chained?

Yes, if compatible. Pending penalties accumulate until countered or drawn.

Does Mirror reflect the last card or the whole attack?

The whole pending attack is reflected to its source when Mirror is compatible.

Are Overdrive rules included?

No. Overdrive is a distinct mode. This manual covers Classic Universe.

Are these physical rules identical to Android?

The core mechanics follow our Classic Universe visual manual. Some physical setup conventions are proposed for this prototype; the exact Power timing and eligibility should be tested before a commercial release.
